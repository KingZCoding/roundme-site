RoundMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

RoundMe is a social app for meeting people nearby. It is run by Dark Matter ITS in New Jersey, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. RoundMe is for people 18 and older.

1. What we collect

WhatDetails
AccountYour email address and password (stored by our sign-in provider, never in plain text), or the name and email from your Google account if you sign in with Google.
BirthdayCollected once, to check you are 18 or older. We calculate your age from it. Other people see only your age, never your birthday.
ProfileDisplay name, gender, who you are looking for, bio, interests, your town, and the optional details you add: height, weight, relationship status, smoking, drinking and education. You choose which of these other people can see.
PhotosProfile photos and photos you attach to posts. Location data embedded in a photo is removed before upload.
LocationYour location, only while the app is open (never in the background), used to find people and posts near you. Before we store it, we round it to the middle of a square about a quarter of a mile across, so we never keep your exact position. It is stored privately and never shown to other people. They see only your town and a rough distance such as "within 5 miles". A post you make remembers where it was made (also rounded), in the same private way.
Content and activityPosts, replies, likes, chat messages, friend requests, swipes, matches and guesses in the Meet game, people you block, and reports you file.
Device and notificationsA push-notification token for your phone and basic device information, so we can send notifications you have allowed.
App error reportsIf the app crashes or hits an error, it sends us a short report: the error message and technical trace, the screen you were on, your app version, update and operating system, and when and how often it happened. Each report carries a random code the app makes the first time it opens. The code is not your account and other users never see it, but it identifies your copy of the app, so we treat it as personal information. We do not store your name or account with the report. We remove email addresses, sign-in tokens and long numbers from the text before keeping it, but this is not perfect, so a report could still contain something personal. We also keep a counter for your account, with no report content in it, to stop one account sending too many reports.
Technical dataOur hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use it

  • To run RoundMe: show you people and posts nearby, deliver messages, matches and notifications.
  • To keep people safe: enforce our Terms, handle reports and blocks, and prevent spam, fake accounts and anyone under 18.
  • To improve and fix the app.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

3. Who can see what

  • Other users see your name, age, photos, town, rough distance, and the profile details you have not hidden. Hidden details are not sent to other people.
  • Posts and replies are visible to nearby people who are allowed to see them. Posts leave the feed after 7 days.
  • Messages are visible to the people in that conversation. They are protected in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Photos are served from public web links. Anyone who has a photo's link can open it until the photo is deleted, even though the link is hard to guess and we do not list photos publicly.
  • People you block cannot see or contact you, and you do not see them.

4. Companies that help us run RoundMe

  • Supabase: hosting, database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you choose it) and Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android notifications.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications. The text of a notification, such as a message preview or someone's name, passes through Expo's and Google's servers to reach your phone. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

These companies may only use your information to provide their service to us. We may also share information when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect someone's safety.

5. How long we keep it

Your information stays while your account is active, with the exceptions in this table. A daily cleanup job removes these items automatically.

WhatHow long
Your stored locationCleared after 30 days without you opening the app. It is stored again the next time you open it. Your town stays on your profile.
Records of location updates we ignored (for example, one that implied impossible travel speed)30 days
Meet game rounds and guesses30 days after the round ends
Notifications90 days (friend requests stay while they are waiting for an answer)
Reports we reviewed and dismissed, and our record of that decision12 months after the decision
Reports that led to action, and our other moderation records (for example, a suspension)3 years after the decision or the record
A one-way scrambled code of the email address of an account we deleted after a serious or repeated violation of our Terms. It cannot be turned back into the address.3 years
App error reports (see section 1)30 days after the same error last happened, so an error that keeps happening is kept while it keeps happening. These reports are not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not remove them earlier. The per-account counter that limits reports is deleted after 1 day, or with your account.

A report still being reviewed is kept until we decide it. We may keep a report longer if it involves a serious safety concern or a legal request.

  • Posts stay until you delete them or delete your account. A post you delete disappears from the app, but its text may stay in our database until your account is deleted.
  • Messages stay until an account in the conversation is deleted. Deleting a conversation in the app only hides it for you; the other person still sees it, and we still keep it. When either person deletes their account, the whole conversation is deleted for both.
  • When you delete your account in Settings > Delete account, your profile, photos, posts, messages and activity are deleted. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time before they expire.
  • Reports and moderation records are kept after an account is deleted, for the times above, including a record of what was reported, so we can protect other people and respond to abuse.

6. Your choices

  • Edit your profile and choose which details are visible, in the app.
  • Turn "Discoverable" off in Settings to stop appearing in Locals and Meet.
  • Control who can message you, and block or report anyone.
  • Delete your account in the app at any time. If you cannot use the app, including because your account is suspended, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it. If your account was suspended for a serious or repeated violation of our Terms, we keep a one-way scrambled code of your email address for 3 years, so the same address cannot be used to sign up again. We cannot turn the code back into your email address.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by email. Depending on where you live (for example, under some US state laws), you may have additional rights; we will honor them.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules in our database, so people only reach what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

RoundMe is only for people 18 and older. We ask for your birthday when you sign up, and we do not knowingly allow anyone younger to use it. If you believe a minor has an account, email us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
