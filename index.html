See the app

Five tabs. One idea: who is around you.

Swipe through the screens. The people and posts shown are made up, for illustration.

Swipe or scroll sideways →

Locals

Your neighborhood, at a glance.

See who is nearby, closest first. Each profile shows only what that person chooses to share, and distances are rough on purpose: you see “under 1 mi” or “1-2 mi”, never an exact spot.

  • Look as far as 5, 10, 25 or 50 miles
  • Hidden profile details are never sent to anyone else

Meet

Swipe. Match. Guess who.

Swipe through people near you and match when the interest is mutual. When someone likes you, play a quick guessing game to find out who. It is an easier way to break the ice.

Chat

Chat when it clicks.

Message your matches and friends. You decide who is allowed to start a conversation with you, and you can block or report in a tap.

And more

Everything is built around who is near you.

Live Feed

Post to the people around you. Reply and like. Posts stay up for 7 days, so the feed always feels fresh.

Friends

Add the people you click with, and keep them close even when you are not in the same place.

Privacy and safety

Built to keep you in control.

Meeting strangers should feel safe. These are not extras, they are how RoundMe works.

  • Your exact location stays private. Other people see your town and a rough distance, never where you are.
  • You choose what your profile shows. Hidden details are not sent to anyone else.
  • Block and report in a tap. Blocked people cannot see or contact you.
  • Adults only. You confirm your birthday when you sign up, and RoundMe is for people 18 and older.
  • No ads and no selling your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
