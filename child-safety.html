RoundMe Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RoundMe is an app for adults to meet people nearby, made by Dark Matter ITS ("we", "us"). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page explains what is not allowed, how we keep minors off RoundMe, how to report a concern, and what we do when we receive one. It applies to everything on RoundMe: profiles, photos, posts, replies and private messages.

1. What is not allowed

You must never use RoundMe to:

  • share, request, create or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated images;
  • groom a minor, or contact a minor for any sexual or romantic purpose;
  • sexualize minors in words or pictures, including "jokes", role play or comments about a child's body;
  • threaten or pressure a minor into sending images or money (sextortion);
  • advertise, arrange or take part in the trafficking or sexual exploitation of a minor;
  • help or encourage anyone else to do any of the above.

Breaking these rules leads to permanent removal from RoundMe and a report to the authorities, as described below.

2. RoundMe is for adults only

Everyone must be 18 or older to use RoundMe. When someone signs up, we ask for their birthday before they can finish setting up a profile or use the app. Anyone who gives a birthday under 18 is stopped there, and their account is permanently marked so it cannot continue. Our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also state that RoundMe is only for adults.

3. How to report a concern

  • In the app: open the profile, post, reply or message, choose Report, and pick Might be under 18 or Inappropriate content. You can also block the person straight away.
  • By email: write to traenknercody@gmail.com. Put "Child safety" in the subject so we see it first.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) first.

You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through the CyberTipline or at 1-800-843-5678. Outside the US, you can find your country's hotline through INHOPE.

4. What we do when we receive a report

  • We review child safety reports before any other kind of report.
  • We remove the content and suspend the account involved. Accounts that break these standards are banned permanently.
  • We keep the relevant records, even if the account is later deleted, so they can be given to the authorities.
  • When we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material, we report it to NCMEC's CyberTipline, as US law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A), and we cooperate with law enforcement.
  • Accounts we believe belong to someone under 18 are removed.

5. Following the law

We follow the child safety laws that apply to us, including the duty to report child sexual abuse material to NCMEC. We respond to valid requests from law enforcement, and we keep these standards up to date as the law and our app change.

6. Child safety contact

Dark Matter ITS has a named contact for child safety who can speak to our practices and handle reports: traenknercody@gmail.com.
